What is QuotaBar?

QuotaBar monitors your Codex usage. It sits in your menu bar and shows how much of your limit you use. You can see your 5-hour and weekly limits at a glance. The app updates in real time to show your remaining capacity. You keep your data on your own computer because the tool works locally.

🚀 How to install

  1. Visit the official release page.
  2. Look for the latest version at the top of the list.
  3. Click the file ending in .dmg or .zip to download the installer.
  4. Open the downloaded file once the transfer finishes.
  5. Drag the QuotaBar icon into your Applications folder.
  6. Double-click the app to start the program.

💡 Features

  • Live Tracking: View your current usage metrics without opening a browser.
  • Local Storage: Your usage history stays on your machine.
  • Limit Alerts: The app shows when you approach your reset time.
  • Minimal Interface: The software uses little memory and stays out of your way.

⚙️ System Requirements

QuotaBar runs on modern macOS systems. It works best on Apple Silicon chips. Ensure your operating system is up to date to receive the latest security patches. The software requires a standard internet connection to pull usage data from your account.
